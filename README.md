# 🚲 Bike Stations Sharing Analysis | Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing bike-sharing station data using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to understand bike station distribution, bike availability, station capacity, and operational status to generate meaningful insights that can support better bike-sharing service management and decision-making.

The project uses data visualization and interactive dashboard features to explore station performance, availability, and geographical distribution.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the total number of bike-sharing stations across different cities.
- Identify the total number of bike stands and available bikes.
- Evaluate the availability of bike stands across stations.
- Identify open and closed bike stations.
- Analyze the geographical distribution of bike stations.
- Examine station facilities such as banking services and bonus availability.
- Identify stations with higher and lower bike availability.
- Provide insights to improve bike-sharing operations and resource allocation.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about bike-sharing stations and their availability.

**Key attributes used in the analysis:**

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Name | Name of the bike station |
| Address | Location or address of the station |
| Bike Stands | Total capacity of bike stands |
| Available Bikes | Number of bikes currently available |
| Available Bike Stands | Number of vacant bike stands |
| Status | Operational status of the station |
| Banking | Indicates whether banking facilities are available |
| Bonus | Indicates whether the station has bonus facilities |
| Contract Name | City or contract associated with the station |
| Last Update Date | Date of the latest station information update |
| Position | Geographical coordinates of the station |

## 📈 Analysis & Visualizations

The Power BI dashboard includes:

1. **Station Overview:** Displays the total number of cities, stations, bike stands, and available bikes.
2. **Bike Stand Availability:** Analyzes available and occupied bike stand capacity.
3. **Geographical Analysis:** Uses map visualization to identify the geographical distribution of bike stations.
4. **Station Status Analysis:** Examines the distribution of open and closed stations.
5. **Station Facilities Analysis:** Analyzes stations based on banking and bonus facilities.
6. **Station-wise Analysis:** Displays station names along with their available bike counts.
7. **Contract-wise Analysis:** Compares bike stand capacity across different city contracts.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

- **Slicers:** Filter the dashboard by station status, update date, bonus availability, and banking facilities.
- **Filters:** Explore specific station categories and operational details.
- **Interactive Visuals:** Visuals respond dynamically to user selections.
- **Geographical Map:** Explore station locations and their distribution.
- **KPI Cards:** Monitor important bike-sharing performance indicators.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Cities and locations with higher concentrations of bike-sharing stations.
- Stations with higher and lower bike availability.
- Differences between total bike stand capacity and available bikes.
- The distribution of operational and closed stations.
- The availability of banking and bonus facilities across stations.
- Locations that may require better resource allocation and operational attention.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Improve bike redistribution between stations to maintain balanced availability.
- Monitor stations with limited available bikes to reduce customer inconvenience.
- Investigate frequently closed or non-operational stations.
- Consider increasing bike stand capacity in locations experiencing higher demand.
- Monitor station availability regularly to improve operational efficiency.
- Use geographical analysis to identify potential locations for future station expansion.
